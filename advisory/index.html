The product

You do not buy conversations. You buy a decision system around one outcome.

The engagement turns a consequential professional or ownership decision into an observable 90-day outcome, a current-state map, explicit trade-offs, and a prioritized execution path. The work stays within what you can control.

Your Arc Map

  1. Outcome and evidence
  2. Current state and constraints
  3. Decision rights and trade-offs
  4. Capability and leverage gaps
  5. Prioritized actions and review dates
  6. Closing evidence and next decision

Fit

Built for consequential work, not general motivation.

This is for you when

  • You own or materially influence the decision.
  • One outcome matters enough to focus on for 90 days.
  • You will do focused work between conversations.
  • You value evidence, confidentiality, and direct feedback.

This is not the right engagement when

  • You want done-for-you implementation or unlimited access.
  • You need legal, tax, investment, recruiting, or therapeutic advice.
  • You expect a guaranteed promotion, revenue, funding, or return.
  • You cannot protect employer or client confidential information.

The 90-day arc

Six conversations. One living map. Decisions that compound.

  1. 01

    Lock

    Define the outcome, evidence, boundaries, and current system.

  2. 02

    Design

    Surface constraints, decision rights, capability gaps, and leverage.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Review evidence, correct the system, and protect the priorities.

  4. 04

    Close

    Record the final state and make the next-stage decision explicit.

Included

A complete private advisory engagement.

  • Diagnostic pre-work
  • Six private 75-minute advisory sessions
  • Arc Map maintained across the engagement
  • No more than three priority actions after each session
  • Bounded asynchronous clarification and accountability
  • Closing review and next-stage recommendation
