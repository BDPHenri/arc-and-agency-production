Diagnosis before prescription

Our Approach

Durable agency is built through better observation, explicit choices, bounded construction, and honest review, not a universal prescription.

AA05
01

01 / See

See the system

Clarify the actual context: obligations, incentives, energy, skills, relationships, resources, and constraints. Separate facts from assumptions and borrowed ambition.

02

02 / Choose

Choose the next question

Name the decision and the evidence that would make it better. Choose a direction small enough to protect existing commitments and meaningful enough to teach you something.

03

03 / Build

Build within capacity

Create the smallest useful practice, system, or artifact. Define time, confidentiality, conflict, and attention boundaries before adding scope.

04

04 / Review

Review the evidence

Examine quality, usefulness, health, learning, and second-order effects. Continue, adapt, stop, or seek qualified advice without turning sunk effort into identity.

05

Remit

What Arc does and does not do

Arc & Agency develops editorial frameworks and supports structured exploration for capable professionals and builders. The work is meant to improve the quality of questions, choices, and systems.

It does not replace legal, financial, tax, medical, or employment advice. It does not prescribe entrepreneurship, promise outcomes, or turn a framework into certainty.

Evidence informs the next cycle. Capacity and boundaries determine its size.
