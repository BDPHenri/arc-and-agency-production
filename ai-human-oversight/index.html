Tools assist; people remain accountable

AI & Human Oversight

AI can extend research and production capacity. It cannot own the context, ethics, confidentiality, judgment, or accountability behind the work.

AA04
01

Useful support

Where AI can assist

  • Research and discovery across appropriately available material.
  • Synthesis, comparison, and structured exploration of alternatives.
  • Drafting and production support under human direction.
  • Checks for consistency, omissions, and questions worth examining.
02

Human responsibility

What remains human-owned

  • Context and interpretation.
  • Ethical choices and confidentiality decisions.
  • Judgment about relevance, quality, and consequences.
  • Final editorial, advisory, and personal decisions.
03

Human gate

A clear gate before use

  • Define the purpose and whether AI is appropriate for the material.
  • Exclude confidential, proprietary, personal, or otherwise restricted data.
  • Review inputs, sources, assumptions, and material omissions.
  • A human accepts, revises, or rejects the output and owns the final decision.
04

Data stance

Confidentiality comes before convenience

Confidential or protected information should not be placed into general-purpose AI tools. Data minimization is the default: use only what is necessary, appropriately available, and suitable for the task.

Arc & Agency does not claim proprietary AI or automated outcomes. AI-supported work remains subject to human review and accountability.

The useful standard is not whether a tool participated. It is whether a responsible human understood and stood behind the result.
