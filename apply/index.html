What the application asks

  1. Your name, professional email, current role, and organization context
  2. Location, time zone, and LinkedIn or equivalent professional context
  3. The one professional or ownership decision you are considering
  4. Why this decision matters now and what you have already tried
  5. What is within your authority to change
  6. Constraints and non-negotiables
  7. Payment route: personally, existing business, or employer sponsorship
  8. Your ability to commit focused work over 90 days
